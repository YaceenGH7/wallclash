How to Play

1

Move or build. On your turn, move your ball one square up, down, left or right — or place one wall.

2

Walls block both balls. Each wall covers two squares. You start with 7 bricks. You can never fully seal a player off from their goal.

3

Jump your rival. If the balls are face to face, hop straight over. If a wall is directly behind them, jump diagonally instead.

4

Reach the other side. In Classic, first ball to the far edge wins. In Star Rush, race to the golden star in the centre.

5

Watch the clock. Each player has 3 minutes. If your time runs out, you lose.
